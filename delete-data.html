Keepr Data Deletion Request

Keepr allows users to request deletion of some or all of their personal data without necessarily deleting their entire account.

How to Request Data Deletion

  1. Send an email to ajsinghindolia@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject: Keepr Data Deletion Request.
  3. Describe which data you want deleted.
  4. We may request reasonable information to verify your account ownership before processing the request.

Do not include your password in your request.

Types of Data

Depending on your request, data may include saved item records, purchase details, receipts, documents, product images, warranty information and service records associated with your account.

Processing and Retention

We will review and process verified requests in accordance with applicable law and our data retention obligations. Data that must be retained by law will be kept only for the legally required period. We will communicate any applicable retention requirements to the requester.
