Keepr Account and Data Deletion

Keepr allows users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Keepr app and sign in.
  2. Open your Profile or Account settings.
  3. Select the Delete Account option, if available.
  4. Follow the confirmation steps to complete the request.

If you cannot access your account or use the in-app deletion option, email ajsinghindolia@gmail.com with the subject "Keepr Account Deletion Request". Please do not email your password.

What Data Is Deleted?

Account deletion is intended to remove your account information and associated data, including saved items, purchase records, receipts, documents, product images, warranty details, service records and other personal records linked to your account.

Data Retention

Data deletion requests are processed through the account deletion process. Information may be retained only where required by applicable law. Any legally required data will be retained only for the period required by law. Data deletion may not recover information that was stored only on a device and never synchronized.
