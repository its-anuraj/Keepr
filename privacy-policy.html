Keepr Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Keepr helps users manage purchased items, receipts, invoices, purchase details, warranties and return dates.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, Keepr may process account information, item details, purchase prices, warranty dates, store information, receipt images and product photographs provided by users.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to organize personal asset records, provide authentication, manage purchase information, and support receipt scanning and warranty tracking.

4. Third-Party Services

Keepr may use Supabase for authentication and data storage and Google Gemini for receipt scanning, depending on the features enabled. Relevant information may be processed by these providers to deliver those features.

5. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Data Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate app features, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

7. Data Deletion

Users can contact us to request deletion of their personal information. Requests will be handled subject to applicable legal requirements and data retention obligations.

8. Children's Privacy

Keepr is not intended to collect children's personal information in violation of applicable laws.

9. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when our privacy practices or app features change. The latest version will be published at this URL.
